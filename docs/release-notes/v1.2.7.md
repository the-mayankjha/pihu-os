Adds the Pihu_v2 spirit experience and improves animation handling across Pihu OS and the Spirit Market.

## Highlights
- Added the Pihu_v2 showcase page with animated previews for idle poses, directional movement, gaze, speech, visemes, listening, and browser automation.
- Added Pihu_v2 to the Spirit Market and linked its showcase from the product and spirit pages.
- Added configurable Market and music keyboard shortcuts.
- Expanded Pihu_v2 animation sequences for expressive speech, listening, and browser automation, with one-shot idle gestures and improved sprite sizing and rendering.
- Marked the legacy spirit as deprecated and surfaced deprecated spirits in the Spirit Market.

## Downloads
- macOS: separate DMG installers for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- Windows: x64 EXE and MSI installers.
- Linux: x64 AppImage and DEB packages.
- Signed updater artifacts and `latest.json` are included for in-app updates.
